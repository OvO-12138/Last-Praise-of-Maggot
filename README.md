# Last-Praise-of-Maggot
Last Praise of Maggot is a creature and genetics expansion for Last Song of Elysian.

The mod introduces Brother Maggot, Sister Maggot, and their juvenile forms, together with a complete genetic system that produces Golden, Yellow-Purple, and Rainbow Maggots. Each variety has its own breeding conditions and dietary requirements. Offspring inherit traits from both parents, while non-rainbow pairings have a very small chance of producing a rare Rainbow Maggot.

Whenever a chicken lays an egg, it has a chance to drop Chicken Poop as well. Chicken Poop can be combined with Biological Residue, a Gold Nugget, or Yellow-Purple Dye to create special feeds. These items are used for breeding, altering the germline genes of basic Maggots, and helping juvenile Maggots grow more quickly.

The in-game guidebook, The Care of Maggots, explains feeding, breeding, growth, and inheritance in detail.

Requires Last Song of Elysian.


落幕区是乐土终曲的生物与遗传扩展模组。

模组加入了区哥、区姐及其幼年形态，并通过完整的遗传系统衍生出黄金区、黄紫区和彩虹区。不同品种拥有各自的繁殖条件与食物需求，后代会根据父母的基因组合继承特征；普通区繁殖时，还有极低概率诞生稀有的彩虹区。

鸡在下蛋时有概率同时掉落鸡屎。玩家可以使用鸡屎、生物残渣、金粒或黄紫染料制作不同种类的特殊鸡屎，用于繁殖、改变基础区的生殖细胞基因，以及帮助幼年区更快成长。

模组内置指南书《区的供养》，详细介绍饲养、繁殖、成长和遗传规则。

需要安装前置模组：乐土终曲。
